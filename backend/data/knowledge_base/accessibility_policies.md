# WorldCup OS Accessibility Guidelines

## Wheelchair Access and Seating
- **Where is wheelchair accessible seating?**
  Accessible seating is located in Sections 102, 115, 128, 204, and 220. Each accessible seat has space for one wheelchair and one companion seat.
- **Which gate is best for wheelchair entry?**
  **Gate C** is the fully flat, ramp-free gate recommended for wheelchair users. It features extra-wide security turnstiles and immediate elevator access to Level 1 and Level 2 concourses.
- **Are wheelchairs available for loan?**
  Yes, complimentary wheelchair escorts are available at all entrance gates. Ask any Guest Services volunteer in a blue vest for assistance. Note: Wheelchairs cannot be rented for the duration of the match; they are for escort purposes only.

## Sensory and Visual/Hearing Support
- **Is there a sensory room?**
  Yes, a quiet Sensory Room is located on the Main Concourse behind Section 112. It features dim lighting, sensory toys, and noise-canceling headphones for guests with sensory sensitivities.
- **Can I get assistive listening devices?**
  Assistive listening systems and closed-captioning devices are available for checkout at the Main Guest Services Desk in the North Plaza (behind Section 101). A valid ID is required as a deposit.
- **Are service animals permitted?**
  Trained service animals (dogs) are welcome. A designated service animal relief area is located outside the South Plaza near Gate B.

## Elevators and Escalators
- **Where are elevators located?**
  Elevators are located near Gate C (serving all levels), the North Plaza entrance (serving levels 1-3), and the East Concourse (serving suites and upper decks).
