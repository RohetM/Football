# WorldCup OS Stadium FAQ

## Match Day General Information
- **What time do stadium gates open?**
  Stadium gates open exactly 3 hours prior to kickoff. Fans are advised to arrive early to avoid security queues.
- **Can I bring bags into the stadium?**
  Only small clutch bags, no larger than 4.5" x 6.5", are permitted. Clear bags up to 12" x 6" x 12" are allowed. All backpacks are strictly prohibited.
- **Is re-entry allowed?**
  No, ticket scanning allows for a single entry. Once you leave the stadium, re-entry is prohibited.

## Food and Beverage
- **Are outside food and drinks allowed?**
  No outside food or drinks may be brought in, except for one factory-sealed plastic water bottle up to 20 oz (500ml) per person, or for medical necessity.
- **Where are concession stands located?**
  Main concessions are in the North Plaza and South Plaza. Hot dogs, burgers, nachos, and local stadium snacks are served there.
- **Is alcohol served?**
  Beer and alcoholic beverages are sold until the 75th minute of the match. Limit of 2 alcoholic drinks per transaction.

## Transportation and Parking
- **How do I get to the stadium by public transit?**
  Metro Line 1 stops at Stadium Station, which is directly connected to the North Plaza via the pedestrian walkway. Metro Line 2 stops at Concourse Heights, a 10-minute walk to the East Concourse.
- **Where can I catch a taxi or ride-share?**
  The designated ride-share pick-up and drop-off zone is in Lot E, located near the South Plaza.
- **Are there stadium shuttle buses?**
  Yes, free stadium shuttle buses operate from the Downtown transit hub to the West Concourse every 5 minutes on match day.
