# WorldCup OS Gate Information

## Gate A (Main Entrance - North Plaza)
- **Location:** Connected directly to the main pedestrian plaza and Metro Line 1 Station.
- **Access:** Direct access to lower levels 100-200, main concourse, and concessions.
- **Accessibility:** Standard steps and escalators. Standard turnstiles. Not recommended for wheelchair users.
- **Hours:** Opens 3 hours before kickoff.

## Gate B (South Gate)
- **Location:** Adjacent to Lot E parking and the taxi/ride-share pick-up zone.
- **Access:** Direct access to South Plaza concessions, service animal relief area, and sections 118-132.
- **Accessibility:** Ramped entry pathway. Standard turnstiles.
- **Hours:** Opens 3 hours before kickoff.

## Gate C (Accessibility Gate - East Concourse)
- **Location:** East Concourse, closest to Metro Line 2 Station (10-minute walk) and disabled parking Lot C.
- **Access:** Serves as the primary accessibility entrance. Direct access to elevators and sections 102-115.
- **Accessibility:** **Recommended Accessibility Gate**. Features flat step-free pathways, extra-wide turnstiles, and adjacent Guest Services desk.
- **Hours:** Opens 3.5 hours before kickoff (30 minutes early to allow easy entry for disabled guests).

## Gate D (VIP & Staff Gate)
- **Location:** West side, next to the Team Bus Arrivals area.
- **Access:** Direct entry to club lounges, luxury suites, media centers, and stadium offices.
- **Accessibility:** Fully accessible elevator access. Only open to credentialed VIPs, suite ticket holders, and staff.
- **Hours:** Opens 4 hours before kickoff.
